What it does

MODULAR PRIVACY INSTRUMENTS
01 · X · NKIP-0002NIP-39 VERIFIED

Tip badges

An XMR badge across timelines, quoted posts, notifications, people lists, profiles and hover cards for anyone who published a Monero address on Nostr or verified it on xmr.bio. Click it to tip from your own wallet; a Xap records the tip on Nostr, separating recipient-confirmed totals from claimed ones.

ɱ 0.004 ✓+0.002 claimedmonero: wallet hand-off
02 · X · NIP-25NOSTR NATIVE

xo, a Monero like

A heart next to the Xap button sends the author a Monero like over Nostr. Hold it (or press Shift+Enter) to like the post on X too. Accounts with xos get a thin orange ring on their avatar and the total in their badge, counted by xmr.bio. Received xos count only from Monero people and can notify you along with your Xaps.

♥ xo 12avatar ring onhold: also like on X
03 · IDENTITY

Your own badge

Four steps: a Nostr key, your X handle, your Monero address, and one tweet that links them. No signer? XMRadar makes a Nostr key for you, kept in this browser, and lists you on xmr.bio (optional) with an OpenAlias wallet name.

✓ Address published & OpenAlias ✓ X account linked via proof tweet ✓ Listed on xmr.bio directory
04 · NKIP-0001 · SWAP GUARDXMR.CLUB FEED

Swap guard & PGP letters of guarantee

On an exchange's order page, XMRadar checks the signed NKIP-0001 receipt, or the operator's letter of guarantee (PGP or MistProof), against the addresses on the page and in what you copy. A verified copy is saved in your browser for disputes. The toolbar shows the site's xmr.club grade, with warnings for open incidents and a warning page before a lookalike site loads.

05 · NKIP-0003

Sign in with Monero

A monero-signin: link opens XMRadar's own window with the site, account, address, callback, and full message. Expired or mismatched links are refused. Your wallet signs; guides for Feather, GUI, Cake, CLI, and RPC included.

06 · GHOSTMAIL

Burner email

A throwaway address in one click for sign-ups. New mail shows up as a notification with verification codes already pulled out.

07 · NIP-07 & NIP-55

Copy posts to Nostr

Use X's share menu (Copy to Nostr), right-click a post on X, Bluesky, Mastodon or any page, or press Alt+Shift+N. Text, images, whole X threads, and quoted shots go to relays via your NIP-07 or NIP-55 signer.

AltShiftNNostr ✓
08 & 09 · COMPOSER WRITING HELPER & CHAT.XMR.CLUB

Writing helper & isolated Chat

Opt-in pen button inside X's composer (desktop and mobile) to proofread or tighten a draft or highlighted selection using local Ollama / LM Studio or your own API key — off by default. Plus Menu → Chat opens chat.xmr.club in Chrome's side panel, Firefox's sidebar, or its own isolated tab on Android.

Ollama · LM Studio BYOK · off by default #lobby #x
STANDALONE ANDROID · GRAPHENEOS READY

X + Monero + Swap Guard in one sandboxed app

Built on Mozilla GeckoView with XMRadar's Firefox MV3 WebExtension bundled inside. Keep X in its own isolated session, open swap sites in a private Swap Guard tab, and sign Nostr events through your own Android signer — with zero Google Play Services, zero Firebase, and zero trackers.

  1. 01
    Isolated compartments — X, Chat and Private Swap tabs run in separate GeckoView cookie jars; external links hand off to your system browser.
  2. 02
    Native NIP-55 signer bridge — Pair with Amber or Ripley Vault for silent Blossom media uploads and explicit post approvals.
  3. 03
    Opt-in live notifications — XMRadar Xap/xo alerts and X/Chat web notifications without FCM or background telemetry.
  4. 04
    No Play Store required — Direct signed arm64-v8a APK from GitHub Releases (works cleanly with Obtainium).

ZERO-TRUST ARCHITECTURE

What it never touches

XMRadar runs locally in your browser or GeckoView shell, with no account. Its own site is asked only for the latest version number; other services are asked only for the features you use. The code is open, so you can check every claim.

privacy.htmlFull policy → KYC-rip/xmradarRead source →
  • ✓
    No Monero keys, no fundsTips open in your wallet, and your wallet signs for Sign in with Monero. XMRadar never sends or holds Monero.
  • ✓
    Your Nostr key, your choiceWith a NIP-07 or NIP-55 signer, it decides what to ask. Without one, XMRadar's IndexedDB key signs only XMRadar events, never your posts.
  • ✓
    xmr.bio listing is your choiceOn by default once verified; untick anytime in Options → Monero identity. Unlist or delete on xmr.bio.
  • ✓
    What an xo showsA public Nostr reaction naming the post, handle, and key (when known). Holding the heart also likes on X.
  • ✓
    No transaction ids in publicTip receipts never include a transaction id, tx key, or payment proof.
  • ✓
    xmr.bio's directory comes wholeDownloaded whole every hour and matched on your device. xmr.bio never learns which accounts you view.
  • ✓
    Discovery is off by defaultLooking up X accounts on relays is opt-in, because relays can see which accounts you view.
  • ✓
    The site you visit stays localxmr.club's signed list is downloaded whole and matched on your device, lookalike warnings included.
  • ✓
    Receipts and letters stay localChecked and saved in your browser only. Keys come from xmr.club's signed list, never from the page.
  • ✓
    Burner mail stays localGhostMail gets only the inbox address, token, and PoW seed — nothing about the page you are on.
  • ✓
    The update check carries no IDOnce a day asks xmradar.app for the latest version number, with no ID or cookies. Switch it off in Options.
  • ✓
    AI writing & tags are opt-inOff by default. Local Ollama/LM Studio keeps text on your device; external providers require your own key.
