What it does

01 · X · NKIP-0002

Tip badges

An XMR badge on X for people who published a Monero address on Nostr or xmr.bio. Click it to tip from your own wallet; a Xap records the tip on Nostr. Totals separate tips the recipient confirmed from ones that are only claimed.

ɱ 0.004 ✓+0.002 claimed
02 · NKIP-0001 · letters of guarantee

Swap guard

On an exchange's order page, XMRadar checks the signed NKIP-0001 receipt, or the operator's letter of guarantee (PGP or MistProof), against the addresses on the page and in what you copy. A verified copy is saved in your browser for disputes. The toolbar shows the site's xmr.club grade, with a warning for open incidents.

04 · Nostr

Copy posts to Nostr

Right-click a post on X, Bluesky, Mastodon or any page, or hover it and press Alt+Shift+N. Text and images go to your relays, signed by your own Nostr signer.

What it never touches

XMRadar runs in your browser. It has no account and no server of its own. The code is open, so you can check every claim below.

KYC-rip/xmradarRead the source →
  • ✓
    No keys, no fundsTips open in your wallet. XMRadar never sends or holds Monero.
  • ✓
    Your signer signsNostr events go through your NIP-07 extension, and you approve each one.
  • ✓
    No transaction ids in publicTip receipts never include a transaction id, tx key or payment proof.
  • ✓
    Discovery is off by defaultLooking up X accounts on relays is opt-in, because relays can see which accounts you view.
  • ✓
    The site you visit stays in your browserxmr.club's signed list is downloaded whole, the same file for everyone, and matched on your device.
  • ✓
    Receipts and letters stay localChecked and saved in your browser only. A letter is asked for only from the order page's own site, and keys come from xmr.club's signed list, never from the page.
  • ✓
    Burner mail stays localGhostMail gets only what the inbox needs (its address and token, and a random proof-of-work seed when it's created), nothing about the page you are on.

Install XMRadar

Not in the Chrome or Firefox stores yet. Download the zip from GitHub Releases, unzip it, and load it from chrome://extensions (Developer mode → Load unpacked), or in Firefox 140+ from about:debugging.

Works with any NIP-07 signer, such as Alby or nos2x, and any Monero wallet that opens monero: links.
